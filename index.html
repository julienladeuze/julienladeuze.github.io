About

I am a Belgian computer scientist trained at the university of Mons and PhD candidate at the ULB (Université Libre de Bruxelles) in QuIC (centre for Quantum information and Communication) under the supervision of Jérémie Roland. As a researcher, my goals are about finding interesting results in the field of quantum computation and, more broadly, theoretical computer science.

Research interests

My research interests are mainly centered around

  • quantum algorithms,
  • quantum computational complexity, and
  • complexity theory in general.
Additionaly, I like programming in various languages. You can find some of my projects on my GitHub profile.

Current work

My current research is about exploiting a novel framework introduced in the paper Taming Quantum Time Complexity.
